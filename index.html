The name means blade.

Dāo (刀) is the Chinese word for a single-edged blade. It is the idea behind every machine we put together: cut out the extras, keep what matters for gaming, and make it reliable.

Dāo Katana is our first pre-built. It pairs a Ryzen 7 processor with a GeForce RTX graphics card on an MSI B550-A PRO board, cooled by a tower cooler and a wall of fans behind a tempered glass panel.

This is a demo website made for a school exhibit. Dāo Computers is a concept brand and nothing here is for sale.
